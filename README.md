# ehligible.github.io

The website of [Eh-ligible](https://github.com/ehligible/ehligible), the countdown to Canadian citizenship:
the support page and the privacy policy, published by GitHub Pages at <https://ehligible.github.io/>.

Plain HTML and CSS, no build step: edit a file, push to `main`, and Pages republishes it.

| Page | Address |
|---|---|
| Support and FAQ (English, French) | <https://ehligible.github.io/> |
| Privacy policy (English, French) | <https://ehligible.github.io/privacy.html> |

The privacy policy's address is in the Google Play listing and in the app (`Links.kt` in the app repo); keep it
stable. The name "Eh-ligible" and its logo are not licensed for reuse.
